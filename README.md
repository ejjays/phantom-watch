Phantom Watch is a free movie and tv app for android that doesn't get in your
way. No subscription, no ads, nothing to set up.

### Get it

- [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ejjays/phantom-watch/releases)
- [w-phantom.pages.dev](https://w-phantom.pages.dev)

### Building

```sh
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug
```

Prerequisites and the full test/lint commands are in [docs/build.md](docs/build.md).

Documentation lives in the [docs tree](/docs/):

### Ethics

Phantom Watch is a player that makes watching publicly available content
easier. It takes zero liability. You are responsible for what you watch and
how you use the app. It hosts nothing; a stream passes from its source to
your screen and nothing from it is kept.
